About HASSION Leather

Your Trusted Leather Manufacturing Partner

Guangzhou HASSION Leather Ltd. is a leather goods manufacturer based in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, China. Founded in 1998, we operate a 3,000㎡ production facility with 200+ skilled workers, supporting overseas buyers with stable OEM and ODM capacity.

We specialize in OEM/ODM leather wallets, passport holders, card holders, key holders, coin cases and related small leather goods. Each project is supported by in-house sampling, material sourcing, hardware customization and logo finishing (embossing, hot stamping, metal plate). We also have experience developing our own leather goods brands, which helps us better understand product design, materials and market needs.

Our products are exported to buyers across USA, Europe, Japan, Korea and the Middle East. We focus on consistent quality, flexible customization and on-time delivery — working with both established brands and growing wholesale buyers.
