About HASSION Leather

Your Trusted Leather Manufacturing Partner

Guangzhou HASSION Leather Co., Ltd. is a professional leather goods manufacturer based in Baiyun District, Guangzhou, China. Founded in 1998, we have grown into a 10,000-square-meter facility with over 400 skilled workers.

We specialize in OEM and ODM production of men's wallets, women's wallets, passport holders, card holders, key cases, coin cases, and other leather accessories. We own two brands — "HASSION" and "Dr. KOFFER".

Our products are exported to customers in Russia, Poland, Japan, Korea, USA, Germany, Italy, France, UK, Dubai, Saudi Arabia, and more. We are committed to quality craftsmanship, on-time delivery, and competitive pricing.
